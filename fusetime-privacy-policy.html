FuseTime — Shutdown Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Publisher: Idylle Soft

1. Scope

This policy explains how FuseTime — Shutdown Timer for Windows handles information. It covers the application supplied by Idylle Soft, including its local settings, diagnostic logs and embedded interface.

2. Information we do not collect

FuseTime does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics SDK or automatic application telemetry. Idylle Soft does not receive your timer settings or cable combination through the application. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone, and does not inspect the contents of your documents. Sound effects are bundled files; playback does not record audio or require an external sound service.

3. Information stored on your device

The application stores your last selected duration, cable combination, force-mode preference, warning preference, interface language, sound preferences and whether you have acknowledged the first-use cable-protection explanation in settings.json. These settings let the app remember your choices. The cable combination is stored in plain text as a preference; it is not an encrypted password or a Windows security mechanism.

Test mode is a session-only choice and is not saved as a preference. It prevents real system actions and is off on the next normal launch. Simulated operations may still appear in local diagnostic logs. Showing the selected action and remaining time in the tray tooltip does not transmit that information.

Diagnostic logs record operation names, timestamps and technical errors to help investigate failures. Error details can include local file paths and therefore your Windows user name. Logs are not uploaded automatically. The app keeps one active log of up to approximately 1 MB and two rotated backups; rotation limits their size, not their age.

The intended application data folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%\IdylleSoft\FuseTime — Shutdown Timer. Logs are in its logs subfolder. An older IdylleSoft\FuseTime.json settings file may be read and migrated if present; the original file is retained unless you use Reset everything or delete it yourself. Windows packaged installations may redirect the physical storage location.

4. Local communication and Microsoft components

The interface, images and sounds are supplied with the application. FuseTime uses a local loopback HTTP server to communicate on your own computer; this is not an Idylle Soft cloud service. The app does not offer cloud synchronization or remote control, and its application code does not intentionally send settings or logs to external servers.

The interface runs in Microsoft Edge WebView2, configured in private browsing mode. WebView2 may maintain runtime data in the application's WebView2 subfolder. Private browsing does not mean all runtime files or diagnostics are absent. Windows, WebView2 and Microsoft Store may process information for their own diagnostics, updates or distribution according to Microsoft's policies and your system settings. Their processing is separate from FuseTime's local preferences. See https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement and Microsoft's WebView2 privacy documentation at https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/data-privacy.

5. Sharing and support

Idylle Soft does not sell your information or share application settings with advertisers. If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, your message and any files you attach. Please remove unnecessary personal information before sending logs. We use support correspondence to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Email is handled through Microsoft Outlook and its service providers. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle support, resolve related issues or meet applicable legal obligations; you may request its deletion using the contact below.

6. Retention, deletion and control

Local preferences remain until changed or deleted. With no active timer, Settings > Options > Reset everything lets you clear saved preferences (including the cable combination and first-use acknowledgement), the old migration settings file and existing application diagnostic logs after confirmation. The interface reloads with defaults and detects the system language again. New settings and logs may be created during subsequent use. Reset everything does not remove WebView2 runtime files, Windows or Microsoft diagnostics, support emails, or copies and backups outside the application data folder.

Logs remain until rotated or deleted, and runtime files follow WebView2's lifecycle. To clear local data, first stop or complete any active protected countdown, close FuseTime fully, then remove its application data folder. If the old IdylleSoft\FuseTime.json file exists, remove it too to prevent old settings being imported again. Minimize to tray does not close the application. Removing settings resets saved choices. Uninstallation may not remove every local or redirected file.

You can use the app without sending information to Idylle Soft. You can also request access to, correction of or deletion of support information you have sent us, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. We cannot remotely read or delete settings that exist only on your device.

7. Security and changes

The application relies on your Windows account's file permissions for local storage. Settings and logs are not additionally encrypted by FuseTime; people or software with access to your account may access them. No storage or communication method is guaranteed completely secure.

We will update this policy when the application's information handling changes and revise the date above. This policy describes version 1.0.0; any future account, analytics or cloud feature will require an updated disclosure before it is introduced.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Idylle Soft at idyllesoft@outlook.com.

Hata ayrıntıları yerel dosya yollarını ve bu nedenle Windows kullanıcı adınızı içerebilir. Günlükler otomatik gönderilmez. Bir etkin günlük yaklaşık 1 MB ile sınırlıdır ve iki yedek günlük tutulur; bu döngü yaşa değil dosya boyutuna göre çalışır.

Veriler için kullanılan uygulama klasörü %LOCALAPPDATA%\IdylleSoft\FuseTime — Shutdown Timer, günlük alt klasörü ise logs olarak belirlenmiştir. Eski IdylleSoft\FuseTime.json dosyası varsa okunup ayarları yeni konuma aktarılabilir; özgün dosya, Her şeyi sıfırla seçeneğini kullanmadığınız veya kendiniz silmediğiniz sürece korunur. Windows paketli kurulumlarda fiziksel saklama konumunu yönlendirebilir.

Idylle Soft bilgilerinizi satmaz ve uygulama ayarlarınızı reklamverenlerle paylaşmaz. Desteğe e-posta göndermeyi seçerseniz e-posta adresinizi, mesajınızı ve eklediğiniz dosyaları alırız. 6. Saklama, silme ve kontrol

Yerel tercihler değiştirilene veya silinene kadar saklanır. Aktif sayaç yokken Ayarlar > Seçenekler > Her şeyi sıfırla bağlantısı, onayınızın ardından kayıtlı tercihleri (kablo kombinasyonu ve ilk kullanım onayı dahil), eski aktarım ayar dosyasını ve mevcut uygulama tanılama günlüklerini temizler. Arayüz varsayılanlarla yeniden yüklenir ve sistem dili tekrar belirlenir. Sonraki kullanımda yeni ayarlar ve günlükler oluşturulabilir. Her şeyi sıfırla; WebView2 çalışma zamanı dosyalarını, Windows veya Microsoft tanılama verilerini, destek e-postalarını ya da uygulama veri klasörü dışındaki kopya ve yedekleri silmez.

Günlükler döndürülene veya silinene kadar kalır; çalışma zamanı dosyaları WebView2'nin yaşam döngüsüne tabidir. Yerel verileri temizlemek için önce korumalı aktif sayacı durdurun veya tamamlanmasını bekleyin, FuseTime'ı tamamen kapatın ve uygulama veri klasörünü silin. Eski IdylleSoft\FuseTime.json dosyası varsa ayarların yeniden içeri aktarılmaması için onu da silin. Tepsiye göndermek uygulamayı kapatmaz. Ayarları silmek kayıtlı tercihleri sıfırlar. Uygulamayı kaldırmak tüm yerel veya yönlendirilmiş dosyaları silmeyebilir.

Idylle Soft'a bilgi göndermeden uygulamayı kullanabilirsiniz. Bize gönderdiğiniz destek bilgileri için geçerli hukuk ve meşru saklama gereksinimleri kapsamında erişim, düzeltme veya silme talebinde bulunabilirsiniz. Yalnızca cihazınızda bulunan ayarları uzaktan okuyamaz veya silemeyiz.
